###   About Me

Just a 16 year old builder from India, turning curiosity into things you can actually see, touch, and experience. I build robots, write software, organize hackathons, and make films basically, I like creating things in both the physical and digital world.  


I've also spent time building a startup and helping early stage teams turn ideas into products and scale them. Always experimenting, always building, and usually chasing the next idea worth bringing to life.

😄 &nbsp;Pronouns: **he/him**
